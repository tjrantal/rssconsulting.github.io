What I do

Real-time signal processing

Low-latency audio and inertial-sensor pipelines: FFTs, spectral analysis and multi-pitch estimation running at interactive frame rates on mobile hardware.

Computer vision and estimation

Camera calibration, sub-pixel image alignment, rolling-shutter modelling and nonlinear least-squares fitting of physical models, with honest uncertainty estimates.

Applied machine learning

Labelling custom data sets, fine-tuning detectors such as YOLOX, and deploying the trained models to phones through ONNX and OpenCV DNN.

Research-grade validation

Methods that are written up, cross-validated between devices and reproducible. Measurement tools that stand up in peer review as well as in the field.
